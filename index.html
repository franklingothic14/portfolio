About

Communication Motion Designer with a background in multimedia journalism, infographics, and video production. Over the years I've created explainer videos, social media animations, and performance-driven ad creatives for NGOs, advertising agencies, and mobile game studios, while also directing and editing short documentary stories about people and everyday life.

Comfortable across the full motion design pipeline — from concept and storyboarding to final animation — using After Effects, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, Photoshop, and Figma. Fluent in Ukrainian, English, and German, currently based in Mannheim, Germany.
